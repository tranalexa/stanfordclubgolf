Fall tryouts are set for **Saturday, September 19** at Stanford Golf Course. Tee times begin at 9:00 AM.

## Who can play

Anyone who has joined the club this year. There is no handicap requirement. If you are new to competitive golf, this is a low-pressure way to see where your game stands.

## Format

- 18 holes, stroke play, from the back tees
- Bring your own clubs and a marker
- Scores determine the travel roster for the first NCCGA regional in October

## How to sign up

Fill out the form on the Membership page and check the box for tryouts. We'll send tee times the week of the event.

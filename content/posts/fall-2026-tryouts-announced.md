**Please read the full [Tryouts overview](/tryouts) before submitting.** Scores are due **October 11**.

## How it works

We assess your **best 18-hole differential** — that can be one 18-hole round, two 9-hole rounds, or a mix. Submit as many rounds as you want; there's no penalty for more scores.

## Rules (short version)

- At least one other player in your group must sign your scorecard
- Double bogey pickup
- Stroke-and-distance for lost ball / OB; one-stroke penalty for hazards

## Tee times

We don't assign official tryout tee times. Use the Stanford course sign-up for early back-9, call the pro shop (up to 3 days ahead from 6:30 AM), or play nearby courses like Baylands.

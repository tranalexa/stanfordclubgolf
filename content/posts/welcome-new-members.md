Thanks to everyone who stopped by our table at the activities fair. Here's the short version of how Stanford Club Golf works.

## Practices

We practice three times a week at Stanford Golf Course. Practices are open to all members regardless of skill level. Show up when you can; there is no attendance requirement.

## Two ways to be involved

1. **Social membership.** Play in practices, socials, and the occasional intramural scramble.
2. **Competitive team.** Qualify through tryouts and travel to NCCGA tournaments each semester.

## Stay in the loop

Follow us on Instagram and check the Schedule page for upcoming events. Questions go to the club email listed on the Contact page.

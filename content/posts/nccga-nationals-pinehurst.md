After a strong fall regional season, Stanford Club Golf earned a spot at the NCCGA National Championship at Pinehurst Resort in Pinehurst, North Carolina.

## The weekend

Two rounds, two courses, and some of the firmest greens most of us have ever putted on. The team arrived Friday for a practice round and a walk through the village, then teed off Saturday and Sunday morning.

## Takeaways

- Pinehurst's turtleback greens punish anything short of a well-struck approach.
- Cold mornings in December mean an extra club on almost every shot.
- Traveling as a team is the best part of club golf. The golf is a bonus.

Thanks to everyone who followed along and to the club officers who made the trip possible. Spring regionals are around the corner.
